08 BEFORE YOU STEP IN

Before your
first good move.

A few things that can make your first visit feel easier.

Is FORM a ladies-only gym?

Yes. This studio concept is designed as a dedicated training space for women, with female-led coaching and a range of ways to move.

Can I start if I’m completely new to the gym?

Absolutely. Begin with a conversation about your experience and comfort level, then learn the basic movements with coaching. You don’t need an existing routine to get started.

Do I have to lift weights?

No. Explore cardio, functional movement, boxing fitness, group sessions or mobility. Your coach can help you find a starting point that matches your interests.

Is personal training part of the membership?

Personal training is a separate option in this demo, starting from ₹4,999 per month. Ask about the coaching format, session frequency and final pricing before choosing a plan.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable workout clothes, suitable training shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Share any concerns about movement or comfort with your coach before you start.

When is the studio open?

The demo opening hours are Monday to Saturday, 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday, 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The timetable above is illustrative and should be confirmed for the live studio.
